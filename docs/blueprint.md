# **App Name**: Dhadhi

## Core Features:

- Symptom Checker: AI-powered symptom analysis and triage suggestions, guiding users on whether to seek professional medical advice.
- Health Q&A: Instant answers to health questions using verified medical content, providing reliable information.
- Medication Information: Detailed information about medications, including usage, side effects, and interactions, sourced from trusted databases.
- Mental Health Chat: 24/7 AI-driven chat support for mental health concerns, offering a safe space for initial support and guidance.
- Myth-Buster: Tool that uses AI to identify and debunk fake health news and misinformation, ensuring users receive accurate health information.
- Nearest Hospital Locator: Map integration to find the closest hospitals and clinics, with navigation support and real-time information.
- Post-Diagnosis Education: Tool providing easy to understand explainations and personalized support for better understanding of the diagnosis. AI personalizes educational materials based on the user's medical conditions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft blue (#79B4FF) to evoke calmness and trust.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F4F7), nearly white, providing a clean and minimal backdrop.
- Accent color: Light green (#90EE90) for call-to-actions and highlighting important information.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif for modern readability.
- Simple, intuitive icons in a line style to represent various health functions and categories.
- Mobile-first responsive design with rounded corners and clear, accessible navigation for users of all ages.
- Subtle transitions and animations to guide users through the app, providing a smooth and engaging experience.
